# operator foundation design: adversarial review

Date: 2026-09-27. Reviewer role: adversarial review of the synthesis on branch `design/foundation`. Design only: no builds, no CI runs, no device actions.

**Read in full:** `README.md`, `docs/design/FOUNDATION.md`, `docs/design/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `docs/adr/README.md` and ADR-0001 to ADR-0015.
**Read to check claims:** research notes 02, 03, 05 and 06 in full; the relevant sections of 01 and 07; `/home/phaseonebig/op13/REPORT.md`; op13's draft build files.

**Tags.** **VERIFIED [n]** means I re-checked it myself against source n (list at the end). **UNVERIFIED** means not checked, followed by what would check it. **INFERENCE** means my own reasoning. `[01§F1.1]` means research note 01, section F1.1.

## 1 Verdict

**Sound with fixes.** The main architecture holds up: a Kotlin main process, a native `:llm` process, a typed executor with risk classes, a route-first loop with per-step grammar, capability scoping and milestones measured on the phone. Most of the facts it depends on check out (§3). It is **not ready for owner acceptance as written**, for three reasons:

- **Two owner decisions are changed without asking the owner:**
  - the JNI layer (R1);
  - decide = CLM-8B, with its encoder and its timing (R2).
- **Four safety holes:**
  - the agent can forge owner input through operator's own notifications (R3);
  - the agent can send data off the phone through other apps' UIs (R4);
  - irreversible UI taps are gated only by a word list and a model guess (R5);
  - the approval gesture does not prove the owner looked at the card, and it overlaps the volume and panic gestures (R6).
- **M1 is measurable and reachable, with two fixes.** The exit criteria are numbers or "record baseline", and M1 can be built in Actions only and run on a USB-attached phone. Two things must be settled first: how the native libraries are packaged (R11), and who unlocks the phone after each reboot test (R14).
